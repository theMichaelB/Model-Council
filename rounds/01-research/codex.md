# Codex proposal: a small, evidence-driven coding harness

Independent research draft. Research date: 2026-11-17.

## Executive summary

Build a thin controller around existing coding agents, GitHub checks, and disposable boat.dev workers—not a new autonomous-agent framework. Prefer bounded tasks, independently verified acceptance criteria, and human approval for consequential decisions.

## Research and proposal outline

1. State of the art and limits of the evidence.
2. Architecture and feature lifecycle.
3. Reuse-versus-build component inventory.
4. AI-built bootstrap and phased delivery.
5. Quality metrics and enforcement.
6. Risks, mitigations, and user decisions.
